Official Privacy Policy

ResumeBloom: CV Maker

Package Name: com.resumebloom.app • Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Privacy Summary: ResumeBloom: CV Maker is built with an offline-first architecture. All resume data, personal details, and photos remain entirely on your local device. We do not maintain user accounts, nor do we host external servers that upload, process, or sell your personal resume information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application ResumeBloom: CV Maker (the "Application", "App"), provided for Android devices. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Data You Provide (Resume Content)

The Application allows you to create, edit, and export professional resumes and curriculum vitae. The information you choose to enter into the Application may include:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone number, physical address, city, country, and website or portfolio links.
  • Professional Details: Professional summary, job titles, employer names, employment dates, responsibilities, and achievements.
  • Academic Background: Degree, school/university name, field of study, graduation year, and grades.
  • Skills & Other Sections: Skills, languages, certifications, projects, and personal interests/hobbies.
  • Profile Photo: An optional profile picture selected from your device's photo gallery.

Storage: All resume data and images are stored exclusively inside the Application's private sandboxed SQLite database on your device. We do not transmit or sync your resume data to any remote cloud servers.

3. Document Generation, Export & Sharing

When you generate a resume, the document (PDF) is created locally on your device via the Android Print and WebView frameworks.

Your resume file never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an action using the Share or Print features. When you choose to share your resume, the file is shared strictly via the native Android Sharesheet with the destination application you choose (such as email, cloud storage, messaging app, or printer).

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The Application is supported by advertising to provide free resume templates and features to all users. We integrate Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs to display advertisements.

These third-party services may automatically collect certain diagnostic and technical information through standard mobile SDKs:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • IP address and approximate coarse location.
  • Interaction data related to ad views and clicks for ad fraud prevention and performance measurement.

Consent Management (GDPR / UK / EEA): Where required by applicable privacy laws, the Application presents a consent dialog provided by Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). Users can review or update their privacy consent choices at any time through the in-app "Ad Privacy Options" located in the settings menu.

For more information on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Application Permissions

The Application requests only the minimal necessary Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely for loading third-party banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob and checking GDPR consent status.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by AdMob to verify network availability before requesting ad impressions.
  • No Storage Permissions Required: The Application uses Android's modern photo picker and secure FileProvider to select profile images and share generated PDF documents without requiring broad access to your device's file storage.

6. Cloud Backups & Data Isolation

The Application explicitly disables operating system cloud backup (android:allowBackup="false") and full-backup extraction rules. This ensures that your private resume details are not copied to third-party cloud backup servers without your consent.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored solely on your physical device:

  • You can edit or delete individual resumes, entries, or profile pictures at any time directly within the "My Resumes" screen.
  • You can immediately and permanently delete all resume data by clearing the Application's storage via Android Settings → Apps → ResumeBloom → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all saved resumes and local app files from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate steps.

9. Security

We value the security of your information. The Application relies on Android's application sandbox security architecture, ensuring that other applications installed on your device cannot access your stored resume content.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will become effective immediately upon posting the revised Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
